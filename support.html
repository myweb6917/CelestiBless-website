Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your iPhone Settings > tap your name at the top > Subscriptions > select ProveMeWrong > Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

How do I delete my account?

Open the App > go to Settings (gear icon) > scroll down > tap "Delete Account". This permanently removes all your data including tasks, progress, and account information.

I didn't receive the email confirmation.

Check your spam/junk folder. If you still can't find it, try signing up again with the same email. The confirmation email is sent from Supabase authentication services.

The app won't load my tasks.

Make sure you have a stable internet connection. The App requires internet access to sync your tasks and generate coach dialogue. Try closing and reopening the App.

Can I change my coach?

Coach selection is set during onboarding. To change your coach, you'll need to delete your account and create a new one. We plan to add coach switching in a future update.

Why is my coach being so harsh?

That's the point. ProveMeWrong uses provocative coaching to hold you accountable. The coach personas are fictional characters designed to motivate through challenge, not comfort. If you find the content distressing, please discontinue use and reach out to us.

I was charged after my free trial. Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Open the Apple "Report a Problem" page at reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund for any recent charge.
